Before we begin

A few useful
starting points.

Can you support more than one service on a project?+

Yes. Our service range covers multiple disciplines. Share your requirements so we can review the interfaces and discuss a coordinated scope.

What do you need to prepare a proposal?+

A description of the work, the site location, an indicative timeline, and any drawings or specifications available. We can discuss the next steps if your scope is still being developed.

Do you work on existing facilities?+

Our services include renovation, upgrades, pipeline maintenance and repair, and electrical improvements. Please tell us about ongoing operations, access restrictions, and any shutdown requirements.
